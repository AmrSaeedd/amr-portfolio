Design Vision & Rigor

Translating High-Level Zoning into Human-Scale Physical Reality

Detailed urban planning establishes the essential link between regional policy goals and tangible built form. This project created a complete blueprint for an integrated residential and commercial district designed to support vibrant neighborhood life.

The plan establishes a clear hierarchy of street sections—distinguishing between commercial boulevards, local residential access ways, dedicated bike corridors, and landscaped pedestrian-only alleys. Each parcel is mathematically defined with coordinate geometry, setbacks, footprint coefficients, and building envelope guidelines.

Special attention was devoted to public realm vitality: pocket parks, tree canopies for sun shading, centralized community service nodes, and coordinated parking lots that minimize visual clutter.

Technical Planning Dimensions

  • 1
    Statutory Planning Controls Comprehensive schedules specifying FAR, lot coverage, maximum building heights, and front/rear/side setbacks.
  • 2
    Street Hierarchy & Circulation Complete right-of-way engineering separating vehicular thru-traffic from tranquil pedestrian corridors.
  • 3
    Infrastructure Integration Coordinated surface stormwater lines, electrical sub-stations, and utility easements.
  • 4
    3D Massing & Scale Volumetric massing studies verifying solar access, street canyon ratios, and pedestrian comfort.
