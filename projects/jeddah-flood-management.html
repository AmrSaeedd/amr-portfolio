Hydrological Context & Problem Statement

Mitigating Destructive Flash Floods through Data-Backed Spatial Interventions

The coastal metropolis of Jeddah is situated between steep granitic mountain ranges to the east and the Red Sea to the west. Sudden, torrential downpours rapidly accumulate into high-velocity flash floods rushing down natural dry-wash wadis directly through dense urban districts, historically resulting in severe casualties and infrastructural failure.

This project utilized high-resolution Digital Elevation Models (DEM) within ArcGIS Pro to model flow directions, flow accumulations, basin boundaries, and peak discharge volumes. By overlaying soil permeability, land use density, and topographical slopes, we generated multi-tier flash flood hazard maps.

Rather than relying solely on costly concrete culverts, the study formulated a resilient urban drainage master plan featuring naturalized wadi bypass channels, permeable retention basins, and multi-functional green floodway parks that absorb stormwater while enriching city life.
