Architectural Concept

Functional Efficiency, Acoustic Buffering & Climate-Responsive Courtyards

Designing a vocational training campus necessitates a rigorous functional zoning program. Industrial machinery and hands-on mechanical, electrical, and carpentry workshops produce high noise and operational vibration, requiring clear acoustic insulation from quiet theoretical lecture rooms, computer labs, and administrative suites.

The campus layout configures these two functional wings around a shared, shaded central gathering courtyard. This courtyard acts as both a vibrant social heart for student interaction and a passive climate-moderating buffer that enhances cross-ventilation.

The full architectural drawing package comprises comprehensive ground and first floor layouts, longitudinal and transverse sections, facade elevations with sun-shading louvers, and a complete exhibition board (shasieh).

Campus Program Elements

  • 1
    Specialized Industrial Workshops Heavy-machinery bays with high ceilings, overhead gantry access, emergency cutoffs, and acoustic buffering.
  • 2
    Academic Instructional Classrooms Optimized natural daylighting, digital technology integration, and flexible lecture configurations.
  • 3
    Courtyard & Microclimate Deep overhangs, perforated brise-soleil screens, and native shade trees mitigating solar gain.
  • 4
    Recreation & Student Amenities Multi-purpose athletic fields, student cafeteria, administrative reception, and bus drop-off zone.
