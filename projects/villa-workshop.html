Design Concept & Technology

Contemporary Geometric Expression Driven by Rigorous BIM Coordination

This residential project explores the intersection of sleek contemporary architecture and high-precision digital authoring using Autodesk Revit. The villa's form is characterized by interlocking rectilinear volumes, cantilevered terraces, and rich textural contrasts between smooth white plaster, warm local stone, and panoramic glass curtain walls.

Developed fully within a Building Information Modeling (BIM) environment, all architectural elements are parametrically constrained and coordinated across plans, elevations, sections, and schedules. This ensured zero spatial collisions between structural framing, floor slabs, and openings.

The residence is organized around a fluid indoor-outdoor living concept: wide sliding glass panels blur the transition between open living/dining halls and a private landscaped rear terrace featuring a private swimming pool, sun lounger deck, and perimeter shade trees.

BIM & Architectural Highlights

  • 1
    Autodesk Revit Parametric Modeling Full 3D digital model with custom wall assemblies, parametric window families, and staircase geometry.
  • 2
    Dynamic Volumetric Cantilevers Upper floor master suite projecting over the ground floor terrace, providing natural passive shading.
  • 3
    Outdoor Living & Water Integration Infinity swimming pool positioned to catch southern light while anchoring private garden vistas.
  • 4
    Photorealistic Rendering Enscape / V-Ray ray-traced perspective renderings studying solar shadows and nocturnal landscape illumination.
