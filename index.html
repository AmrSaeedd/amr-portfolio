Professional Profile

Integrating Spatial Data with
Human-Centered Urbanism

I am Amr Saeed, an Urban Planner, Landscape Designer, and GIS Specialist. My work unites the analytical precision of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) with innovative architectural and urban design principles to shape sustainable, resilient, and vibrant communities.

From conducting machine-learning-driven environmental fragility assessments for coastal sectors in Matrouh, to formulating regional strategic growth alternatives for Sohag and detailed urban district designs, I approach every project with contextual awareness, technical rigor, and aesthetic excellence.

Spatial & GIS Analytics

ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, spatial multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA), remote sensing, NDVI/NDWI indexing, and K-Means clustering.

Strategic & Detailed Planning

Regional growth models, statutory planning codes, block parceling, multimodal mobility corridors, and utility coordination.

Landscape Architecture

Public parks, water-sensitive lagoon design, arid microclimates, botanical nurseries, civic plazas, and watercolor presentation.

BIM & Visual Design

AutoCAD 2D drafting, Revit parametric modeling, 3D architectural perspective rendering, and Adobe Photoshop composition.
